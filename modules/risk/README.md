# Risk Management Module

## Overview

The Risk Management Module provides comprehensive risk management capabilities for the AI Banking GRC Platform, supporting risk identification, assessment, mitigation, and monitoring across the organization.

## Features

### Risk Dashboard
- Real-time risk statistics
- Risk heatmap visualization
- Risk trend analysis
- Basel III compliance monitoring
- High-risk alerts
- Pending review notifications
- Recent risk activity

### Risk Register
- Complete risk repository
- Advanced search and filtering
- Risk categorization
- Status tracking
- Risk scoring
- Historical tracking

### Risk Assessment
- Likelihood and impact scoring
- Velocity and persistence assessment
- Inherent risk calculation
- Residual risk calculation
- Risk matrix mapping
- Assessment history

### Risk Mitigation
- Mitigation planning
- Treatment strategy selection
- Action tracking
- Control implementation
- Progress monitoring

### Risk Heatmap
- Interactive visualization
- Risk distribution mapping
- Color-coded risk levels
- Drill-down capabilities

### Basel III Dashboard
- Capital adequacy monitoring
- CET1 ratio tracking
- Tier 1 ratio monitoring
- CAR compliance
- Leverage ratio tracking

## Supported Frameworks

| Framework | Version | Authority |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Basel III | 2024 | Basel Committee |
| ISO 27001 Risk Assessment | 2022 | ISO |
| NIST RMF | 1.1 | NIST |
| SBP Prudential Regulations | 2024 | State Bank of Pakistan |
| COSO ERM | 2024 | COSO |
| ISO 31000 | 2018 | ISO |
